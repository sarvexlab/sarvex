---
title: Account & team
description: Your organization, team, notifications, and billing — shared across every store on your account.
---

**Account** is where you manage your organization — everything here is shared across every store on your account, not scoped to any one of them.

- **Business & team** — your business name and locale (used for billing, scheduling, and compliance across every store), the account owner, and your team. Everyone on the team can sign in and sees every store; only the account owner can invite teammates. Invited members sign in through your identity provider — no Sarvex password. Changing the account owner is concierge-assisted in V1.
- **Notifications** — task emails (every new task that needs you, prefixed by store) and a weekly digest (one paragraph across all stores, sent Mondays), each toggled independently.
- **Billing** — your plan, payment method, and invoices, managed through Stripe. Upgrade any time from your Stripe portal, prorated automatically; downgrading to Self-serve goes through your concierge. Card details go straight to Stripe — Sarvex only sees the last four digits.

With more than one store, opening **Account** from the store switcher shows every storefront as its own card, each running its own independent Path. This list is a summary, not a workspace — the actual work happens on a store's own Path. Adding or removing stores is operator-driven in V1, not self-serve.

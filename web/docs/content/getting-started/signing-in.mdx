---
title: Signing in
description: How to sign back in after your first invite, and how account/store pickers work.
---

Go to [the dashboard](https://app.sarvex.io) and sign in with your Google account (or identity provider). Sarvex authenticates through your provider and never stores a password.

If your account has more than one store, signing in takes you to an account picker — the same "Your storefronts" list from [Account & team](/using-the-dashboard/account-and-team) — where you choose which store's Path to open. A store switcher then stays in the console header, showing each store's blocked status and task count so you can jump between stores at any time.

---
title: Getting access
description: Sarvex isn't self-serve yet — how to request access and get your first invite.
---

Sarvex isn't self-serve yet — there's no public sign-up form.

1. [Contact the Sarvex team](https://sarvex.io/contact) (or email us) to request access.
2. We set up your merchant account and send a sign-in invite to your admin email address.
3. Open the invite and sign in with your Google account (or your configured identity provider). No Sarvex password to create.
4. Your first sign-in takes you straight to store setup.

---
title: What syncs automatically
description: Product, stock, and price changes sync to Sarvex automatically — near real-time, with a safety-net re-check.
---

Once paired, the plugin watches for changes on your store and reacts to:

- Adding or updating a product
- Updating stock quantity
- Stock status changes (in stock ↔ out of stock)

Each sends a lightweight signal to Sarvex — just the product identifier and what changed — and Sarvex pulls the full, current product details from your store. It's automatic and near real-time; nothing to trigger.

If a change doesn't show up right away, there's nothing to fix — Sarvex periodically re-checks your whole catalog as a safety net, so a dropped signal (a missed webhook, a brief outage) gets caught and corrected on its own.

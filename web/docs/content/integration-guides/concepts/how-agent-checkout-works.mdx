---
title: How agent checkout works on your store
description: Sarvex handles negotiation and checkout end to end — your store only confirms live stock, shipping, tax, and the resulting order.
---

Sarvex handles negotiation and the checkout session end to end. The one point it talks to your store live during checkout is to confirm real-time stock, shipping, and tax, and to record the resulting order — the same information any other checkout flow would get.

Your store never hands Sarvex a raw payment credential — Sarvex only ever sees a reference to the payment method.

Completed orders land in your store's normal order list, and also show up in the Sarvex console under **Orders**, linked back to the checkout session that created them.
